Privacy Policy for Terp Match

Last updated: September 2026

Terp Match (operated by Winkelmann Interpreting Services, Inc.) connects sign language interpreters, mentors, and mentees. This application provides a professional networking tool for the interpreting community.

1. Information We Collect

We collect profile information you provide during account creation, including your name, credentials, professional experience, mentorship interests, and contact details. We also collect reports and blocking actions submitted through the platform.

2. How Information is Used

Your profile information is used exclusively to facilitate mentoring matches, professional networking, and app security within the community. We do not sell or monetize your personal data.

3. Community Standards & User Content

Users must adhere to the Terp Match Code of Conduct. Harassment, misrepresentation of credentials, and unauthorized sharing of confidential assignment details are strictly prohibited. In-app features allow users to block and report abusive behavior immediately.

4. Account Deletion Requests

Users may request complete deletion of their account and associated profile data by contacting the administrator directly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or account inquiries, please contact Winkelmann Interpreting Services, Inc.
